# 7. CASE STUDY — SUNDARAM FINANCE

This must be a prominent section on the homepage.

Headline:

# AI Hiring Agent for Sundaram Finance

Subheadline:

> Turning candidate screening into an intelligent, automated hiring workflow.

Explain the challenge:

Sundaram Finance was struggling to recruit sales executives because many candidates left within the first month of joining.

The challenge was not simply generating more candidates. The organization needed to identify candidates whose expectations, motivation, communication, experience and understanding of the sales role were better aligned with the actual requirements of the job.

Solution:

Kartavya built an **OpenClaw-based AI Hiring Expert**.

The AI agent:

- Screens candidates
- Conducts structured conversations
- Asks role-specific questions
- Asks difficult and probing follow-up questions
- Evaluates candidate responses
- Identifies inconsistencies and potential concerns
- Assesses motivation and expectations
- Produces structured candidate assessments
- Helps recruiters focus on the most relevant candidates

Architecture:

OpenClaw + LLM + hiring methodology + structured assessment + enterprise workflow

Outcome:

> Streamlined the hiring pipeline, reduced manual screening effort, improved candidate-job alignment, and significantly reduced early employee churn.
